Drag a card, get a reviewed pull request.

Nolzu is a kanban board that starts your coding agent on your own machine, with your own login. It works with Claude Code, Codex, Cursor and Antigravity, and keeps its data on your machine. The client is free.

```bash
npx nolzu@latest
```

It needs Node 22.16 or newer. Nolzu is tested on macOS. On Linux and WSL 2 it starts with a warning until their test round.

- [nolzu.com](https://nolzu.com) shows what it does, and the [quickstart](https://nolzu.com/docs/quickstart) takes you to your first card.
- [nolzu/nolzu](https://github.com/nolzu/nolzu) is the client's source, public under [FSL-1.1-ALv2](https://github.com/nolzu/nolzu/blob/main/LICENSE). Each release becomes Apache 2.0 two years after it ships.
- The [roadmap](https://github.com/orgs/nolzu/projects/1) shows what comes next. A thumbs up on an issue counts as a vote.
- Found a bug? [Open an issue](https://github.com/nolzu/nolzu/issues/new/choose).
